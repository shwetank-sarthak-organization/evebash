How to use this file (people and Claude sessions alike):
- Read it before changing a plan's price or storage, picking a provider plan, or changing how photos,
  videos or guests use a paid service.
- Money is per month unless it says "a year". ₹95 = $1. Rupee figures are rounded.
- Infra only. Salaries, marketing and tax are not in these numbers. Apple Developer and Google Play
  fees are left out. Razorpay's fee (2% + GST) is charged to the customer, so it's ₹0 to us.
- "Measured" = read from our database, bucket or a real bill. "Estimate" = a guess to replace with
  real numbers after launch (section 10). Guest numbers are the biggest estimate.
- To update: re-check section 10 first (every table depends on it), recompute with section 12,
  change the date above and add a line to the CHANGE LOG at the end.
- Bugs go in evebash_issue.txt, planned work in evebash_todo.txt. This file only records costs.

1. SUMMARY
=====================================================================

- A typical customer (Pro plan, 120 GB stored, one 200-guest event a month) costs us ₹7,511 a month
  when they're our only customer, ₹224 at 100 customers and ₹152 at 1,000.
- Cost to serve one more typical customer: ₹114 a month, ₹80 of it storage. Shared bills spread
  over more customers, so the cost per customer falls towards this.
- About 13 Pro customers pay all the infra bills. At 1,000, infra takes 23% of the Pro price.
- Storage is about half the bill at every size. The new photo and video pipelines cut processing by
  63%, but cost to serve fell only about 20% (₹142 to ₹114) because storage prices didn't change.
- 1,000 GB plan at ₹25,000 a year: breaks even at about 10 customers who fill it, then makes
  ₹7,700 to ₹9,100 per customer a year (about ₹16,500 if customers average 500 GB).
- Biggest open risk: Cloudflare's terms on video and large files (section 11).

8. EACH SERVICE IN PLAIN WORDS
=====================================================================

Example: 100 customers who each fill 1,000 GB. In a month that's 100 TB stored, 8.3 TB uploaded,
3.24 lakh photos, 26,667 video minutes, 833 events and 1.67 lakh guests (half on the app).

EXPO - ₹18,905 a month
  What it does: normally a fix means a new app version, store review and waiting for people to
  update. Expo sends the fix straight to phones; the app picks it up the next time it opens.
  How it charges: by how many different people download a fix in a month. Someone who downloads
  five fixes counts once. A guest who installs the app for one wedding still counts.
  How we got the number: 1.67 lakh guests, half on the app = 83,333; half of those download a
  fix = 41,667. Starter would be $19 + 38,667 x $0.005 = $212, so Production ($199, 50,000 people)
  is cheaper.
  What we control: we only pay if we publish fixes this way.
    People downloading a fix   Bill a month
    0                          ₹0
    5,000                      ₹2,755
    20,000                     ₹9,880
    41,667                     ₹18,905
  After we publish one, new installs also download it until the next store release, so one fix
  can keep counting for weeks. Use it for urgent fixes and follow with a store release.

SUPABASE - ₹12,474 a month
  What it does: the master register. Who has an account, which events exist, which photos belong
  to which event, who can see what. Photos sit in Backblaze; Supabase keeps about 15 KB of details
  per photo. It also handles sign-in. App guests must sign in; website guests can view without
  an account, so they don't count as users.
    Part            Why                                                        A month
    Pro plan        Free pauses a project after a week with no activity and    ₹2,375
                    has no automatic backups
    Bigger server   Many guests opening galleries at once (Large, my estimate) ₹9,500
    Extra disk      Database about 58 GB; Pro includes 8 GB                    ₹599
    Monthly users   83,433, under the 1 lakh included                          ₹0
  Server sizes, extra over Pro's credit: Small ₹475, Medium ₹4,750, Large ₹9,500 a month. Large
  was chosen for about 28 events a day; it isn't load tested. Start smaller and move up when real
  load needs it.
  Big jump: monthly users pass 1 lakh at about 120 full customers; each extra person is $0.00325.
  At 500 full customers that's about ₹98,000 a month.

VERCEL - ₹3,800 a month
  What it does: sends the website pages when someone opens evebash.com.
  How it charges: by seats (team members who can publish the site). 2 x $20 = $40.
  Why pay: Hobby (free, used today) doesn't allow commercial use.
  Why it stays low: photos and videos come through Cloudflare, not Vercel. At 100 customers about
  83,333 website guests use 125 GB and 33 lakh page requests; Pro includes 1,000 GB and 1 crore.
  Extra charges start at about 300 full customers; at 500 the bill is about ₹5,000.

RAILWAY - ₹1,900 a month
  What it does: runs the backend, which handles requests from the app and website all day. Heavy
  photo and video work runs on Modal and RunPod, which keeps Railway cheap.
  How it charges: like an electricity meter, with a monthly minimum you pay even if you use less.
  How we got the number: $4.13 today + 83.3 lakh requests (50 per guest) x $1.50 per 10 lakh
  (estimate) = about $17, under Pro's $20 minimum, so $20.
  Usage passes $20 at about 130 full customers, then adds about ₹12 a month per extra customer.

BACKBLAZE - ₹66,993 a month: 100 TB at $7.05 per TB a month. Biggest bill, because paid customers
  keep everything. Only storing less lowers it.
MODAL - ₹15,236 a month: photos ₹9,725 (₹0.03 each) + Find You ₹8,333 (₹0.05 per guest) + ₹28
  idle - ₹2,850 free credit.
RUNPOD - ₹4,267 a month: 26,667 video minutes x ₹0.16. Rate from 3 test jobs.
CLOUDFLARE - ₹4,940 a month: $5 plan + about 16.7 crore media requests (1,000 per guest) after the
  first 1 crore. It doesn't charge for data delivered, which is why its terms are a risk.
SENTRY - ₹2,755 a month: flat $29 Team plan. The free plan is for one person with a small error limit.
QSTASH - ₹641 a month: 2 messages per photo + 1 per video minute = 6.75 lakh messages at $1 per lakh.
HOSTINGER - ₹300 a month: placeholder price for the domain and email.

11. RISKS AND OPEN QUESTIONS
=====================================================================

- Cloudflare's delivery rules: its terms say video and mostly large files need its paid products,
  and it may limit accounts that break this. 1,000 typical customers would deliver about 50 TB a
  month through it. If media moves to Bunny: ₹23 to ₹142 per typical customer a month.
- Plan limits are web-only (issue #6): event uploads can go past a plan's storage. Any GB past
  the plan is storage we pay for and the customer doesn't.
- Guest numbers are estimates. No real wedding has run yet. Doubling guests adds ₹71 per typical
  customer; halving saves ₹15.
- Real uploads may look different: sizes come from test data. A video minute stores about 0.12 GB.
- Thin RunPod billing history: the rate comes from 3 billed jobs. Video is under 5% of typical
  cost to serve, so the effect is small.
- Only one copy of customer files. A second copy adds ₹80 per typical customer.
- Supabase server sizes are a judgement call, not load tested. Each size up to Large adds ₹475 to
  ₹4,750 a month (more above Large) and could come sooner.
- Dollar bills: almost every bill is in dollars. Each ₹5 on the dollar adds about 5%. Bank and
  card currency fees are not counted.
- GST on foreign bills: Backblaze charges 18% GST, which these numbers leave out. If we can't
  claim it back: +₹14 per typical customer a month, +₹121 per full 1,000 GB customer. Other
  foreign bills may add it too.
- GST on our prices: not decided whether plan prices include GST. The plan pricing code adds none,
  and the policy text says prices "may include or exclude" it. If included, we keep price ÷ 1.18.
- Launch plan switches assumed in every table: Supabase Pro, Vercel Pro (Hobby is non-commercial),
  Cloudflare Workers Paid (issue #132).
- The dashboard steps Supabase, Railway and Sentry by customer count, sized for a 120 GB customer.
  For heavy customers it reads a little low (Heavy is about ₹29 a month low at 1,000). Section 7
  and section 12 step by load instead.


=====================================================================
12. HOW TO RECOMPUTE THE NUMBERS
=====================================================================

For n customers who each store S GB (all copies counted), per month:

  Uploads per customer        U = S ÷ 12 GB
  Guests                      G = n x (U ÷ 10 events) x 200 guests
  App guests                  50% of G; web guests are the rest
  Photos                      n x U x 38.9
  Video minutes               n x U x 3.2
  Load                        n x (S ÷ 120) x (10 ÷ GB per event), in typical customers

  Backblaze    n x S x $6.95 per TB per 30 days (x 30.44 ÷ 30 for a calendar month)
  Modal        photos x ₹0.03 + G x ₹0.05 + $0.29 idle - $30 credit (never below 0)
               If Modal does video: + video minutes x ₹0.24 + network over 1 TiB at $0.04 per GiB
               (56 MB per video minute)
  RunPod       video minutes x ₹0.16
  QStash       (2 x photos + video minutes) ÷ 1 lakh x $1
  Cloudflare   $5 + (G x 1,000 requests - 1 crore) x $0.30 per 10 lakh
  Supabase     $25 + server + (database GB - 8) x $0.125 + (app guests + n - 1 lakh) x $0.00325
               database GB = 0.1 + n x S x 38.9 x 15 KB
               server, over the $10 credit, by load: Small $5 from 50, Medium $50 from 250,
               Large $100 from 750, XL $200 from 1,500, 2XL $400 from 2,500
  Railway      the larger of: $5 ($20 from load 250), or $4.13 + G x 50 requests x $1.50 per 10 lakh
  Vercel       2 seats x $20 + data over 1,000 GB x $0.15 + requests over 1 crore x $2 per 10 lakh
               (web guests x 1.5 MB, web guests x 40 requests)
  Sentry       $29 from load 25, else $0
  Expo         people downloading an update = 50% of app guests; cheapest of Free ($0 up to 1,000),
               Starter ($19 with 3,000, then $0.005 each), Production ($199 with 50,000, then
               $0.005 each)
  Hostinger    ₹300
